**Thread title:** Aion 4.8 NA - Optional Poeta & Ishalgen Skip (Both Factions)

Sharing my optional starter-zone skip for **both Elyos and Asmodians** on Aion 4.8 NA / Beyond Aion.

New characters get a full-screen choice: **Play Poeta / Play Ishalgen** or **Ascend to Sanctum / Ascend to Pandaemonium**.

- Choose your advanced class and begin at level 10.
- Complete 41 eligible Poeta quests or 47 eligible Ishalgen quests, including Ascension.
- Receive skipped quest item rewards and alternative choices through mail, plus quest Kinah, titles and cube expansion.
- Teleport and bind to your faction's capital.
- Start the capital ceremony with **Leah** (Elyos) or **Heimdall** (Asmodians), bypassing the starter-zone NPC step. Ceremony rewards are earned normally when completed.
- Start your class-specific dispatch to **Verteron** or **Altgard**.
- Keep the welcome screen visible until dismissed; duplicate claims are protected.

**GitHub:** https://github.com/AngelShade/aion-poeta-skip

Includes complete server source, version-checked client build/install/restore tools and documentation. Client files and artwork are generated locally from your own matching client.

**Supported setup:** English Aion 4.8 NA 64-bit; the current standalone package requires client and GameServer on the same computer. Read the integration notes when combining mods.

Build and automated checks passed, including both faction menus at three resolutions and database progression/recovery for all 11 Asmodian classes. Fresh in-game testing of the standalone package is still needed. Feedback and bug reports are welcome!
